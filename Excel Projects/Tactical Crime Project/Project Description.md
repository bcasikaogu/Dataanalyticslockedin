# Sherlock Bryan reporting for duty (sir yes sir)
On this segment I'll be doing data analysis on, you guessed it, a tactical crime report for three years between 2021-2023.

During this period they were 2500 crimes committed involving 7410 people. So on average 800 crimes a year give or take

### The crimes in question were as follows:
- Possession of Weapons

- Bicycle Theft

- Robbery and Mugging

- Theft from a Person

- Drugs and Illegal Substances

- Burglary

- Shoplifting

- Vehicle Crime
  
- Criminal Damage and Arson
  
- Public Order

- Anti-Social Behaviour
  
- Violence and Sexual Offences
  
And the people involved in these crimes were one of the following
- Accomplice
- Informant
- Offender
- Victim
- Witness
